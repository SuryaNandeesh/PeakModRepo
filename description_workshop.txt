Peaking It / TreeSword

This mod currently adds:

- A new town NPC: the Vocal Vendor
  - Moves in after the world flag "DownedDoppelganger" is set (debug command available for testing).
  - On each world load, randomly appears as one of four Vocaloid-inspired variants (Miku, Rin, Kaito, Meiko) and keeps that look for the whole session.
  - Sells mod-exclusive gear, including the Barkblade and Shadow Essence.

- Barkblade
  - A melee sword that flicks out a slow, spinning leaf projectile on every swing.
  - Sold by the Vocal Vendor for 15 gold.

- Shadow Essence
  - A mod-only crafting material intended to drop from a future Doppelganger enemy.
  - Currently also sold by the Vocal Vendor.

- Doppelganger-themed weapons and projectiles (for future use)
  - Void Cleaver: a voidy melee sword (NPC-only for now).
  - Shadow Sling + yoyo projectile: a shadowy yoyo with a faint dark trail.
  - Null Shot: a small shadow projectile used by the Null Shot Bow and intended for the future Doppelganger.
  - Null Shot Bow: a ranged weapon that fires Null Shot projectiles instead of arrows.

All custom items and NPCs use placeholder vanilla sprites until custom art is added.
